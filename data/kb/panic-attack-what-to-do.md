## What a panic attack feels like
A sudden surge of fear with a pounding heart, chest tightness, shortness of breath, sweating, trembling, dizziness, tingling hands, and often a belief that you are dying or losing control. It usually peaks within about ten minutes and then fades, even though it feels endless while it lasts.

## In the moment
Remind yourself what this is: a false alarm from your body's threat system. It is frightening but not dangerous in itself. Do not fight it, since resisting tends to feed it. Slow your exhale (in for four, out for six), drop your shoulders, and ground yourself by pressing your feet into the floor and naming things you can see. Stay where you are if it is safe to do so.

## Afterwards
Drink some water, rest, and go easy on yourself. Avoid concluding that you must now avoid the place where it happened, since avoidance keeps the fear alive.

## When to get checked
If it is your first attack, if symptoms are unusual for you, or if you have chest pain that spreads to your arm or jaw, pain with exertion, or breathlessness that does not settle, get medical help. Call 112 in an emergency. Repeated attacks are common and respond well to treatment, especially CBT, so mention them to a doctor.
