---
title: Supporting a friend who is struggling
topics: helping others, friend, support, third party
---
## Start by listening
You do not need to fix anything. Find a private moment, say what you have noticed ("You have seemed really down lately, and I am worried about you"), and let them talk. Avoid rushing to advice, comparing with your own experience, or saying "just think positive".

## Ask directly if you are worried about their safety
If they hint at hopelessness or not wanting to live, ask plainly: "Are you thinking about suicide?" Asking does not put the idea in their head, and many people feel relief at being asked. Stay calm and take the answer seriously.

## What to do next
Do not promise to keep it secret if their life may be at risk. Encourage them to contact Tele-MANAS (14416, free, 24x7), a doctor or a counsellor, and offer to sit with them while they call. If you think they are in immediate danger, do not leave them alone, remove obvious means of harm if you can, and call 112.

## Look after yourself
Supporting someone is draining. You are not their only support and not responsible for their recovery. Talk to someone you trust, or a helpline, about how you are coping.
