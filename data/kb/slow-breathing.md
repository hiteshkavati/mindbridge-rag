## Why slow breathing helps
When you are anxious your breathing gets quick and shallow, which makes the physical sensations (tight chest, dizziness, tingling) stronger. A long, slow exhale nudges your body toward its calming response. It does not remove the cause of stress, but it can lower the intensity enough to think clearly.

## Box breathing
Breathe in through your nose for a count of four, hold for four, breathe out gently for four, hold for four. Repeat for two to five minutes. Keep your shoulders loose and let your belly rise rather than your chest.

## If holding your breath feels uncomfortable
Skip the holds. Breathe in for four and out for six to eight. The longer exhale is the part that matters. If you feel light-headed, return to normal breathing for a minute and try a gentler pace.

## A note on health
If you have asthma, a heart condition or another breathing problem, check with your doctor before doing breath holds.
