---
title: Racing thoughts when you are trying to sleep
topics: sleep, overthinking, anxiety
---
## Why nights feel worse
At night there are fewer distractions, so unfinished worries get louder. Trying harder to sleep usually makes it worse because effort raises alertness.

## Before bed
Do a brain dump earlier in the evening: write what is on your mind and a short list for tomorrow. Once it is on paper, your mind has less reason to keep rehearsing it. Keep the pad by the bed in case something comes up later.

## In bed
Do not check the clock. Try a slow body scan or progressive muscle relaxation, which gives the mind something neutral to follow. If thoughts pull you in, label them ("planning", "worrying") and gently return to your breathing or body. Aim for rest rather than sleep; lying quietly is still useful and lowers the pressure.

## If it keeps happening
Combine this with a steady wake-up time and scheduled worry time during the day. If poor sleep goes on for weeks, speak to a doctor, since CBT for insomnia works well.
