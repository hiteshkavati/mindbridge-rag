## The idea
Trying to stop worrying usually backfires. A more workable approach is to give worry a container: fifteen to twenty minutes at a set time each day when you are allowed to worry as much as you like.

## How to run it
Pick a regular slot, not close to bedtime. During the day, when a worry shows up, jot a few words in a notes app and tell yourself you will deal with it at worry time. At the slot, go through the list. For each item ask whether it is something you can act on. If yes, write the next small step (send the email, check the fee deadline, book the appointment). If it is outside your control, write that down and move on to the next one.

## What to expect
The first few days it can feel artificial. Many people find the pull of worries outside the slot weakens after a week or two, because the mind learns they will not be lost. If worry is constant, takes over most of your day, or comes with physical symptoms and avoidance, it is worth talking to a professional.
