## What is really going on
Procrastination is rarely laziness. It is usually a way of avoiding an uncomfortable feeling, such as fear of doing it badly, boredom or confusion about where to start. Giving yourself a hard time tends to add to the discomfort and makes avoidance more likely.

## Shrink the first step
Define the smallest version of the task you can do in five minutes: open the document and write a rough heading, or list three bullet points. Write the next physical action, not the whole project. Starting is the hard part; momentum usually follows.

## Use time boxes
Work for 25 minutes on one thing with notifications off, then take a short break. Decide beforehand what "done for now" looks like.

## Reduce friction
Put the materials out, close unrelated tabs, and tell someone what you will finish by when.

## When to look deeper
If you struggle with almost everything, have felt low or anxious for weeks, or have lifelong trouble with focus, it may be linked to anxiety, depression or attention difficulties, and a professional assessment can help.
