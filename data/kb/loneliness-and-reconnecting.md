## It is common and it is not a flaw
Loneliness is about the gap between the connection you have and the connection you want. It is especially common after moving to a new city, changing jobs or finishing college, when daily contact with people disappears.

## Small steps
Send a message to one person you have lost touch with. Say yes to low-pressure invitations. Join something that repeats weekly, such as a sports group, a language class or a hobby club, because familiarity builds through repeated contact, not one big evening.

## Give it time
New friendships in a new place often take months, not weeks. Do not judge a group by the first meeting.

## Online and offline
Online communities can help, but try to move at least some contact into real life or voice and video calls.

## Be kind to yourself
Loneliness can make you assume others do not want you around, which leads to withdrawing, which deepens it. If it lasts for months, comes with low mood, or you feel unable to reach out, talking to a counsellor can help.
