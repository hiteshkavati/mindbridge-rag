## What it is
MindBridge is an information assistant that answers questions about everyday stress, mood, sleep, work and relationships. Its answers are based on a small library of written self-help material, and it shows which passages it used.

## What it is not
It is not a therapist, a doctor or a crisis service. It cannot diagnose conditions, recommend medication or replace professional care. It can make mistakes, so use your own judgement and check anything important.

## In an emergency
If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 112. For emotional support at any hour, Tele-MANAS (14416) is free and open 24x7.

## Your privacy
Conversations are kept in memory for the length of a session and are not written to disk by default. Message text is not recorded in logs. You can delete your session at any time.

## Staying in touch with people
Talking things through here can help you sort out your thoughts, but the people in your life matter. Consider sharing how you feel with someone you trust.
