## Why it stings
Social feeds show other people's highlights next to your behind-the-scenes. Promotions, new offers and holidays get posted; rejections, doubts and bad weeks do not. Comparing your whole life with their best moments is not a fair test.

## Notice the effect
Pay attention to how you feel after scrolling. If you reliably feel smaller, restless or anxious, that is useful information.

## Practical changes
Set a time window for apps instead of checking all day, and keep them off your phone's home screen. Mute or unfollow accounts that trigger comparison; you can do it quietly. Replace some scrolling with contact: send a message to a friend or comment on a post thoughtfully.

## For job hunters
Announcement posts show outcomes, not the number of rejections behind them. Try posting your own work in progress, and use the platform for contacts and learning rather than rankings.

## If it is more than that
If comparison leaves you hopeless or you spend hours online to avoid your feelings, talk to someone, since it can be a sign something else needs attention.
