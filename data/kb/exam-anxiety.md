## Some nerves help
A moderate level of arousal sharpens focus. The problem starts when worry gets so strong that you cannot concentrate or your mind goes blank.

## Before the exam
Break the syllabus into small chunks and schedule them. Practise recalling material from memory rather than rereading, and do past papers under timed conditions. Protect sleep in the last few nights, because a tired mind retrieves worse than a slightly under-revised one.

## On the day
Do a couple of minutes of slow breathing before entering. Skim the whole paper first and begin with questions you can answer. If your mind goes blank, move on to another question and come back, and take a slow exhale before restarting.

## Afterwards
Avoid long post-mortems with friends. You cannot change the paper that is done, but you can use the time to prepare for the next one.

## When to ask for help
If anxiety is causing panic attacks, constant dread for weeks, or you cannot study at all, speak to a college counsellor or a professional. Support exists for students and asking is common.
