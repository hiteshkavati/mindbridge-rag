## Why basics matter
Sleep, movement, food and light shape mood and stress more than most people expect. They are not a cure for anxiety or depression, but neglecting them makes everything harder.

## A minimum viable day
On bad days, aim for three anchors: get up at roughly the same time, eat at least one proper meal, and go outside for a short time. Anything beyond that is a bonus.

## Movement
A brisk 20 to 30 minute walk most days is linked with better mood and sleep. Pick something you can actually keep up, such as walking, cycling or a game with friends.

## Light and food
Get daylight in the morning. Eat regular meals and drink enough water. Heavy alcohol use tends to worsen anxiety and sleep.

## Keep it light
You do not need an elaborate routine. Choose one or two habits and stick to them for a few weeks before adding more. If your mood or energy stays low despite looking after these basics, see a doctor, since there may be something else going on.
