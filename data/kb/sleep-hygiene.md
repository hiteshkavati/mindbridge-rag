## Anchor your wake-up time
A consistent wake-up time, including weekends, does more for sleep than any single trick. Get daylight within an hour of waking if you can.

## Evening basics
Cut caffeine after early afternoon, since it can stay in your system for many hours. Avoid using alcohol to fall asleep; it fragments sleep later in the night. Dim lights and reduce screens for the last hour, or at least lower brightness and avoid stimulating content. Heavy exercise is better earlier in the day.

## Make the bed for sleep
Use the bed mainly for sleeping. If you have been awake for around twenty minutes, get up, sit somewhere dim, do something quiet like reading a book or stretching, and go back when sleepy. Lying there frustrated teaches the brain to associate bed with alertness.

## When to see a doctor
Occasional bad nights are normal. If trouble sleeping lasts several weeks, affects your daytime functioning, or comes with loud snoring, gasping at night, or low mood, see a doctor. CBT for insomnia is an effective treatment.
