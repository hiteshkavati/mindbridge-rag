## Nerves are normal
A pounding heart and dry mouth before speaking in public are the same arousal that powers a good performance. The aim is not to remove it but to use it.

## Prepare in the way you will perform
Practise aloud, standing, with a timer, not just in your head. Rehearse the first minute until it is automatic, since a smooth start settles the rest. For interviews, practise answers to common questions aloud with a friend.

## Calm the body
Do slow breathing for a couple of minutes beforehand: in for four, out for six. Relax your shoulders and unclench your jaw. Some people find it helpful to reframe the sensation as excitement ("I am keyed up and ready") instead of fear.

## During
Pause when you lose your place; a pause feels long to you but natural to the audience. Look at a friendly face. If you stumble, carry on without apologising.

## Afterwards
Note two things that went well before listing what to improve. If fear is so strong that you avoid speaking altogether, a gradual exposure plan or professional support can help.
